Recommendation

Conditionally choose PLTR 30%, LLY 30%, AMZN 20% and MSFT 20% for the $24,000 school portfolio. The expanded research retains this baseline: three relatively strong price trends plus Amazon’s operating catalyst. It does not establish a higher-return replacement. It is a judgmental tournament choice—not a statistically optimized portfolio or a demonstrated short-term edge. All four report earnings during the holding window; PLTR alone represents 74.1% of recently measured portfolio variance.

No portfolio can guarantee a win or gains every day. A four-stock, eight-session contest is dominated by surprises and entry prices. This basket deliberately accepts meaningful loss risk to retain earnings upside. Without competitors’ holdings and a defensible future-return model, a numerical probability of winning would be invented.

The four investment cases

PLTR 30% · $7,200

Catalyst: November 2 after close; call at 5 p.m. Company schedule.

Bull case: Q2 revenue grew 93%, with U.S. commercial revenue up 149%. Another upward revision could validate the rally. Management’s Q3 revenue guide is $2.160–2.164 billion; merely reaching it is not necessarily enough. Bear case: the valuation bar is demanding even after rapid growth. Stock compensation and the distinction between bookings and committed revenue matter. Disconfirmation: a weaker forward growth trajectory, softer commercial conversion or margin erosion. A strong reported quarter can still trigger valuation compression. Q2 SEC release.

Valuation basis: the latest filed July 27 count is 2.403 billion actual Class A/B/F shares—not a fresh October count or the weighted diluted EPS denominator. At $198.84, the equity-value proxy is $477.8 billion, about 58.6× the $8.154 billion full-year revenue-guide midpoint; not enterprise value. SEC share counts. Retaining 30% is an intentional concentrated competition wager, not evidence-optimized sizing.

LLY 30% · $7,200

Catalyst: October 29 results; call at 10 a.m. The announcement does not specify a release hour. Company schedule.

Bull case: Q2 sales rose 48% to $22.97 billion; volume increased 60%. Management raised full-year revenue guidance to $85–87 billion. Sustained demand and supply execution could support another favorable outlook. Bear case: realized pricing fell 13%; volume must keep offsetting price pressure. Disconfirmation: a guidance reduction, launch/supply disappointment, or pricing deterioration without compensating growth. This is a different business driver, not a safe-haven guarantee; no new clinical approval is assumed during the contest. Q2 company results.

Foundayo contributed $98 million in Q2, only about 0.43% of company sales. Oral-launch headlines need patient retention, dose/pricing mix and margin evidence; injectables remain the main revenue driver. Separate operating changes from newly acquired IPR&D charges when assessing guidance. Product and guidance tables.

AMZN 20% · $4,800

Catalyst: October 29; results call at 5 p.m. Company schedule.

Bull case: Q2 AWS revenue grew 37% to $42.2 billion, offering a specific monetization test alongside retail operating leverage. Management’s Q3 operating-income guide is $22.5–26.5 billion and assumes no energy-derivative remeasurement. Bear case: trailing free cash flow was negative $7.6 billion, and investment gains inflated net income. Disconfirmation: AWS deceleration, operating-income disappointment or capital spending without improving cash conversion. Focus on operations rather than investment-mark-driven EPS. Q2 company results.

Operating-profit quality: Q2 included a $551 million unrealized energy-derivative gain within Technology and Infrastructure, primarily affecting AWS—about 2% of consolidated operating income. Removing it gives roughly $26.91 billion. This is distinct from the investment gains in net income, and is noncash; do not assign the entire adjustment to AWS as an exact normalization. Compare Q3 core profit with the no-remeasurement guide. 10-Q Energy Contracts note.

Amazon’s 20-session trend is weaker than Microsoft’s (+1.19% versus +4.59%). It remains for its distinct retail/AWS operating test, not a validated short-horizon edge. Holding both increases cloud/AI spending overlap rather than providing full diversification.

MSFT 20% · $4,800

Catalyst: October 28 after close; no unverified exact release minute is assumed. IR calendar; company timing FAQ.

Bull case: FY26 Q4 revenue rose 18% to $90 billion. Continued cloud demand and paid-AI conversion could support the outlook. Bear case: investment and depreciation raise the execution bar, while discrete items benefited EPS. Disconfirmation: cloud/outlook weakness without better cash conversion. Q4 results.

Use the September restatement: Q4 Azure growth is 42%, formerly 43%. The new FY27 segments are Agents and Infra, and Devices and Consumer. Recast Q1 Azure guidance is 44–45% constant currency; compare the next report on that basis, not July’s definition. Total-company revenue guidance remains $89.85–90.95 billion. These are reporting changes, not a guidance cut. September SEC update.

Q1 capex still exceeds $50 billion. Extending building useful lives from 15 to 25 years changes future lease classification; the lower reported capex expectation does not mean lower underlying investment. Reconcile cash payments, cloud margin and delivered capacity before calling it improved efficiency. July CFO outlook.

Before the opening and after each report

  1. Confirm the rules: paper versus real money, November 5 exit, fractional shares, permitted rebalancing, treatment of cash/fees, and how the school records opening prices. Exactly four common stocks and the $2 minimum must still hold at entry.
  2. Refresh the evidence: check overnight issuer filings, event changes, premarket gaps and spreads. These October 26 closes are not executable October 27 quotes. Reassess if a material disclosure, timing change or opening gap consumes the thesis; do not automatically chase or substitute.
  3. Size from the accepted fill: use each budget divided by its actual contest entry price. With whole shares only, floor that quotient and record residual cash; exact $24,000 deployment generally requires fractional shares or a different permitted rule. Do not fabricate shares or a fill to make totals fit.
  4. Record thesis changes, not just red or green prices: compare reported guidance, margins and cash conversion with the specific tests above. If rebalancing is allowed, act only under the contest rules; otherwise treat this as a fixed basket through the confirmed exit. No automatic averaging down is assumed.

How to read the evidence

The expanded quantitative universe is 40 stocks plus SPY and QQQ: 23 original stocks and 17 additions. The same 252 sessions span October 24, 2025–October 26, 2026, with 251 daily returns per ticker; 4,284 new bars supplement the unchanged 6,300 original bars. Additional F/VTRS/HUM checks were qualitative-only and do not inflate that count. Retrieval ran October 27, 06:53–07:32 UTC. Split-adjusted returns exclude cash dividends; completeness, OHLC ordering and date alignment were checked.

Single-stock volatility uses the sample standard deviation of daily log returns × √252. Portfolio volatility uses weighted arithmetic daily returns and their covariance; it is a fixed-weight diagnostic, not literal buy-and-hold horizon volatility. Historical eight-session returns run from the first session’s open to the eighth session’s close, then combine constituent returns using initial dollar weights without rebalancing.

There are 245 overlapping windows but only 31 stride-eight nonoverlapping windows; even those need not be independent. Adjacent overlapping windows share seven sessions. This is not 245 independent trials. Today’s hand-selected universe, one year of data, limited earnings episodes and omitted spreads, fees and slippage constrain inference. These fixed-weight replays are not an actual strategy backtest, an out-of-sample alpha test or evidence of a winning probability.

Underlying raw data, scripts, manifests and calculation outputs are retained locally and available on request. The original calculations, MSFT sensitivity and 17-stock extension were independently reconciled. The extension contains 37 unique portfolio recipes after removing repeated baseline/XOM rows—not 40 independent trials. Sources and calculations do not remove forecast uncertainty.
